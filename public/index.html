Safety & Privacy Consent

GuardianLink requires access to your location and microphone to verify safety states and dispatch alerts to contacts. Read our full Privacy Policy for safety details.

GPS Location Trails: Coordinates are captured and logged *only* when an SOS alert is active or during a commute. Continuous background tracking is disabled.
Voice Distress Listening: When enabled, speech is analyzed locally. Raw audio is never sent or stored on the server; only transcripts of active alerts are processed.
Data Isolation: You retain complete control. You can request account deletion, modify trusted contacts, and erase incident logs at any time.
